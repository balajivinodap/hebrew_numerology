# Family Numerology

A private app in which family members calculate numerology numbers, research people, and keep that research with interpretations from their own books and text database.

## Language

### People

**Member**:
A family member who signs in to the app.
_Avoid_: user, account

**Subject**:
Any person whose numbers and life are researched in the app. A Subject can be a Member, a relative, a contact, or a public figure.
_Avoid_: person, profile, client

### Research

**Research Entry**:
The research on one question about one Subject. Members can add to it on later days, and each Finding carries its own date.
_Avoid_: research, report, session

**Finding**:
One item inside a Research Entry, tagged by its origin: calculated, Member note, web, source, or AI.
_Avoid_: fact, insight, data point

### Sources

**Source**:
A book or text database that a Member supplies as a reference for interpretations.
_Avoid_: document, file, reference

**Passage**:
A cited piece of text from a Source.
_Avoid_: chunk, excerpt, quote

**Rule**:
An approved statement that ties a number to a meaning or a prediction, with the Passages that support it.
_Avoid_: interpretation, number feature, meaning
